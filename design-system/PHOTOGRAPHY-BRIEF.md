# To The North — Photography brief

Shot list for Waseem. Every image slot on the site, what it needs, and why. 42 frames total.

The site applies one treatment only: a dark legibility gradient, `rgba(10,10,10)` 30% at the top to 75% at the bottom. That means **the bottom third of every hero frame will be darkened by us**. Expose so shadow areas keep detail; do not bake in your own vignette or gradient. No presets, no grain, no duotone, no HDR look. Natural colour, warm where the light is warm.

## Global requirements

- **Format:** RAW plus full-resolution JPEG, sRGB.
- **Minimum resolution:** 6000px long edge for heroes, 4000px for cards and portfolio, 3000px for portraits.
- **Horizon:** level, always.
- **Captions:** exact location with every file, e.g. "Kharfaq Lake, Gilgit-Baltistan". Credit runs as "Photo: Waseem Photography" throughout.
- **Crops:** shoot loose. Several slots are cropped to more than one ratio by the layout, so leave room on all four edges.

---

## 1. Homepage hero — HOME-01 (1 frame)

- **Where:** the full-viewport opening image of the site. The single most important photograph on the platform.
- **Ratio:** full-viewport cover. Shoot 3:2; must survive a 21:9 crop through the centre.
- **Subject:** Attabad Lake, Gilgit-Baltistan. The turquoise water against bare rock.
- **Composition:** the headline ("The mountains are waiting. So is the road.") sits **lower-left** with a gold CTA under it. Keep the lower-left quadrant quiet: water, shadow, or clean rock, no bright detail. Key subject (the lake colour, the peaks) in the upper two-thirds and right half. Our gradient darkens the bottom 40%.
- **Light:** golden hour, low side light raking the ridges. Water should still read turquoise.
- **Min resolution:** 8000px long edge if possible; 6000px floor.

## 2. Destination heroes — DEST-HERO-01…06 (6 frames)

- **Where:** full-width hero of each destination detail page (upcoming).
- **Ratio:** 21:9 crop from a 3:2 master.
- **Composition (all six):** destination name sits lower-left over the gradient, so keep the lower-left quiet and darkenable. Horizon in the upper third. One clear subject per frame.
- **Subjects:**
  - **DEST-HERO-01 Hunza Valley:** Rakaposhi from Duikar/Eagle's Nest at dawn, valley floor still in shadow, summit lit.
  - **DEST-HERO-02 Skardu:** Katpana cold desert dunes with the Indus behind, late afternoon, long shadows on the sand.
  - **DEST-HERO-03 Naltar Valley:** a Naltar lake (Satrangi) at midday when the blue-green water is at full saturation, pines framing.
  - **DEST-HERO-04 Kalash Valleys:** Bumburet village lanes and wooden houses under walnut trees, spring, morning light.
  - **DEST-HERO-05 Neelum Valley:** the river bend at Keran, green terraces on both banks. Soft overcast is fine here.
  - **DEST-HERO-06 Kumrat Valley:** deodar cedar forest with dawn mist along the Panjkora river.

## 3. Destination cards — DEST-CARD-01…06 (6 frames)

- **Where:** the destination grid on the homepage and Destinations index. Same six places as above but **different frames**, tighter and simpler; card and hero must not repeat.
- **Ratio:** 3:2 master that survives a near-square (4:5 to 1:1) centre crop. Card renders roughly square.
- **Composition:** destination name overlays **bottom-left** on our gradient, so bottom 25% simple. A region tag overlays **top-right**, so no critical detail in that corner. One subject, centre-weighted.
- **Light:** consistent across the set so the grid reads as a family. Prefer morning or golden hour.
- **Min resolution:** 4000px long edge.

## 4. Story heroes — STORY-HERO-01…06 (6 frames)

- **Where:** the 60vh hero at the top of each story page. Title overlays lower-left.
- **Ratio:** 21:9 crop from 3:2.
- **Composition:** lower-left quiet for the title and category tag. Gradient darkens the bottom.
- **Subjects (matched to the editorial calendar):**
  - **STORY-HERO-01 Fairy Meadows:** Nanga Parbat's Raikot face from the meadow, evening alpenglow, pine silhouettes low in frame.
  - **STORY-HERO-02 The KKH:** the road itself as a ribbon, ideally the stretch below the Passu cathedral ridge, morning.
  - **STORY-HERO-03 Passu Cones:** first light hitting the spires, foreground still blue-dark.
  - **STORY-HERO-04 Deosai Plains:** the open plateau in summer wildflower, morning, big sky.
  - **STORY-HERO-05 Rakaposhi base camp:** Minapin glacier and the north face from the base camp meadow.
  - **STORY-HERO-06 Kharfaq Lake:** dawn reflection, absolutely still water.

## 5. Story cards — STORY-CARD-01…06 (6 frames)

- **Where:** story grid on the homepage and Stories index. Same six stories, different frames from the heroes.
- **Ratio:** 16:9, no overlay text on the image (the card carries its own title below), so these can be freer.
- **Composition:** one strong subject, centre-weighted, reads at 400px wide. Detail shots work well here: boots on the trail, a jeep at a bend, tea at camp.
- **Min resolution:** 4000px long edge.

## 6. Partner heroes — PARTNER-HERO-01…03 (3 frames)

- **Where:** hero of each TTN Certified partner page (upcoming).
- **Ratio:** 16:9.
- **Composition:** the partner in their real environment; honest, not promotional. Lower third darkenable.
- **Subjects:**
  - **PARTNER-HERO-01 Karakoram Expeditions:** a small trekking group on Baltoro moraine, Trango or K2 behind.
  - **PARTNER-HERO-02 Eagle Nest Hotel:** the terrace at Duikar at sunrise, Rakaposhi and Lady Finger on the horizon.
  - **PARTNER-HERO-03 Northern Jeep Service:** a 4x4 fording a stream on the Deosai track, spray frozen.

## 7. Founder portrait — FOUNDER-01 (1 frame)

- **Where:** About page, "Our story" split layout.
- **Ratio:** 3:4 vertical.
- **Subject:** Waseem in the field, camera in hand. Not posed against a wall; on the road or at a viewpoint.
- **Composition:** eye level, subject occupying the centre and lower two-thirds, mountains soft behind. Minimal space above the head.
- **Light:** open shade or low sun. No flash.
- **Min resolution:** 3000px on the short edge.

## 8. About portfolio — ABOUT-PF-01…06 (6 frames)

- **Where:** "From the portfolio" grid on the About page. This set proves the archive; it should show range.
- **Ratio:** 3:2.
- **Subjects, one each:** a road frame (KKH switchbacks), a water frame (Attabad or Neelum river), a peak frame (Rakaposhi or Nanga Parbat), a people frame (locals or travellers, candid), a village frame (Kalash or Hunza old town), a forest frame (Kumrat cedars).
- **Composition:** free. No overlays. Pick the six you would hang on a wall.
- **Min resolution:** 4000px long edge.

## 9. Team portraits — TEAM-01…04 (4 frames)

- **Where:** About page team row, rendered as 110px circles.
- **Ratio:** 1:1, head and shoulders, face centred (circular crop).
- **Light and background:** open shade, consistent background tone across all four so the row reads as a set.
- **Min resolution:** 3000px.

## 10. Community feature images — COMM-01…03 (3 frames)

- **Where:** above featured community quotes on the Community page.
- **Ratio:** 16:9.
- **Subjects:** candid traveller moments that could plausibly be a member's photo: camp at Rush Lake, a loaded jeep before departure, a group at a KKH viewpoint. These should feel less polished than the editorial set. Phone-quality authenticity is acceptable here if the moment is right.
- **Min resolution:** 3000px long edge.

## 11. Destination photo strips — DEST-STRIP-01…06, 4 frames each (24 frames)

- **Where:** the horizontal photo strip on each destination detail page.
- **Ratio:** 3:2. No overlay text; each carries a location caption below.
- **Subjects:** four frames per destination that the hero and card do not cover — details, activity, texture. The prototype labels each slot with its intended subject (e.g. Hunza: Baltit Fort from the bazaar, Attabad boats, Husseini bridge, apricot blossom). Follow the labels in the prototype or substitute stronger frames from the same place.
- **Min resolution:** 4000px long edge.

## 12. Partner galleries — PARTNER-GAL-01…03, 4–5 frames each (13 frames)

- **Where:** the "On the ground" staggered gallery on each partner detail page.
- **Ratio:** 3:2 masters; the first frame of each set runs wide and is cropped to 16:9, so keep its subject centre-weighted vertically.
- **Subjects:** the business at work, documentary not promotional — follow the slot labels in the prototype (Karakoram Expeditions: Baltoro crossing, Concordia camp, the Hushe office, mess tent, Trango from Urdukas; Eagle Nest: terrace at first light, a room, Rakaposhi from the garden, dinner service; Northern Jeep: Deosai track, stream crossing, workshop yard, Sadpara viewpoint).
- **Min resolution:** 4000px long edge.

## 13. Story article images — STORY-INLINE-01A…03A and STORY-ESSAY-01…03 (6 frames)

- **Where:** inside the story articles. Each story carries one inline 3:2 image in the prose column and one full-bleed essay break that runs edge to edge of the viewport.
- **Inline (3:2):** a supporting frame from the story — the treeline reveal at Fairy Meadows, the gorge wall on the KKH, a tripod on the Passu river flat. Location caption below.
- **Essay break (very wide crop):** the single strongest frame of each story; it is cropped to roughly 2:1 and darkened only by its grey caption strip below, not by any gradient. Shoot 3:2, keep the subject in the central band.
- **Min resolution:** 6000px long edge for essay breaks, 4000px for inline.
