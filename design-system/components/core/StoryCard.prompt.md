Editorial story / blog card. Full-width 16:9 image, grey category pill, Playfair title, 2-line excerpt, author · date · read-time meta. Hover lifts the border and shifts the title to gold. The tag is deliberately not gold: cards repeat in grids, and nine gold tags in one section would defeat the one-gold rule. The gold `Tag variant="gold"` is reserved for a single featured story per page.

```jsx
<StoryCard
  image="photo.jpg"
  tag="Trekking"
  title="Three days on the Fairy Meadows trail"
  excerpt="The walk up from Tato is steep, but the view of Nanga Parbat is the reward."
  author="Waseem"
  date="Jun 4"
  readTime="6 min"
/>
```
