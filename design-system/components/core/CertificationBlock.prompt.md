The signed TTN endorsement, used at the top of every Partner detail page. TTN Certified is a human verdict, not a directory listing — this block makes the judgement visible. Gold 1px border on TTN Deep (the section's one gold element), certified badge + since line, then the verdict in Playfair Display italic 20px white, signed in Inter 13px grey.

Write verdicts in first person plural, TTN voice: specific, honest, no sales language. Say what the team actually experienced.

```jsx
<CertificationBlock
  since="March 2022"
  verdict="We have sent our own families here. The rooms are simple, the sunrise is not, and in seven years they have never let a traveller of ours down."
/>
```
